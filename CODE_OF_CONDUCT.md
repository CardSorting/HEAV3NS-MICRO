# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free engineering environment for all contributors and maintainers of **HEAV3NS-MICRO**.

- Focus technical discussions on empirical verification, architectural invariants, and reproducible benchmarks.
- Respect the blameless postmortem and Proof-Before-Mutation principles defined in [`SOUL.md`](SOUL.md) and [`docs/CONSTITUTION.md`](docs/CONSTITUTION.md).
